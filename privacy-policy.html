⚡ Privacy Policy

SF Instant Finder - Chrome Extension

Last Updated: December 2024

🔒 In Short: We collect NO personal data. We don't track you. We don't send data anywhere. Everything happens in your browser.

📋 Overview

SF Instant Finder is a Chrome extension designed to help Salesforce users quickly search and access metadata like Flows, Apex Classes, Custom Objects, and 35+ other categories. This privacy policy explains what data we access and how we use it.

🚫 Data Collection

We collect ZERO personal data. This extension operates entirely within your browser and does not transmit any information to external servers.

🔍 What We Access

The extension only accesses the following to function:

  • Salesforce Session Cookie (sid): Read from your browser to make authenticated API calls to your own Salesforce org
  • Active Tab URL: To detect if you're currently on a Salesforce page
  • Tab Information: To open selected metadata in new browser tabs

✅ What We DON'T Do

  • ❌ We do NOT send any data to external servers
  • ❌ We do NOT track your browsing history
  • ❌ We do NOT store any personal information
  • ❌ We do NOT share data with third parties
  • ❌ We do NOT use analytics or tracking cookies
  • ❌ We do NOT collect email addresses
  • ❌ We do NOT display advertisements

⚙️ How It Works

  1. You open a Salesforce page in Chrome
  2. You click the SF Instant Finder icon
  3. Extension reads your Salesforce session cookie
  4. Extension makes API calls DIRECTLY to your Salesforce org
  5. Results are displayed in the extension popup
  6. All processing happens in your browser - nothing leaves your device

🔐 Permissions Explained

Here's why we request each permission:

activeTab

To detect the current tab URL and check if it's a Salesforce page.

scripting

To read Salesforce session information from the active tab.

tabs

To open your selected search results in new browser tabs.

cookies

To read the Salesforce session cookie (sid) required for authenticated API calls to your Salesforce org.

host_permissions

To make API calls only to Salesforce domains:

  • *.salesforce.com
  • *.force.com
  • *.lightning.force.com
  • *.my.salesforce.com
  • *.salesforce-setup.com
  • *.visualforce.com

💾 Data Retention

No data is retained. Everything is processed in real-time in your browser. No search history, no cached results, no user data.

🌐 Third Parties

This extension does not use any third-party services, analytics tools, tracking libraries, or external APIs (except your own Salesforce org for API calls).

🔒 Security

All communication with Salesforce happens via HTTPS. Your session cookie never leaves your browser except to make direct API calls to your Salesforce org.

👶 Children's Privacy

This extension is designed for Salesforce professionals and is not intended for children under 13.
